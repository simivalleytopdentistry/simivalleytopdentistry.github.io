Meet the Founder

Dr. Saeed Bigdeli, DDS

Dr. Saeed Bigdeli is dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered dental care in a warm and welcoming environment. A proud graduate of the University of Southern California (USC) School of Dentistry, he combines advanced clinical expertise with genuine compassion for every patient he serves.

His personal journey has shaped his caring approach to dentistry. At just 14 years old, he moved to a new country on his own, an experience that instilled in him values of resilience, kindness, and empathy. These qualities remain at the heart of his practice philosophy: helping every patient feel comfortable, confident, and cared for.

Throughout his career, Dr. Bigdeli has been actively involved in community outreach programs, including AYUDA, the USC Mobile Clinic, and a General Practice Residency at Mount Sinai School of Medicine in New York, experiences that reinforced his belief that dentistry is about improving lives, not just treating teeth.
